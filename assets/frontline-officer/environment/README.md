# Frontline Officer Environment Art

Top-down foliage and battlefield scatter art for the first-town war slice.

## Runtime Notes

- `frontline_foliage_sheet_256x128.png` is an 8-frame, 64 px sprite sheet for bushes and tree-line clumps.
- `frontline_environment_props_256x128.png` is an 8-frame, 64 px sprite sheet for debris, craters, broken barriers, cable boxes, rocks, and log stacks.
- `battlefield_scars_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for craters, burned vehicles, collapsed cover, wire, rubble, and disabled support props.
- `tree_line_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for dense tree-line and concealment edge pieces.
- `village_war_props_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for ruined village and improvised war props.
- `mud_water_terrain_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for muddy ground, road ruts, puddles, and waterlogged craters.
- `smoke_fire_fx_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for smoke and fire effects.
- `ruined_town_scatter_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for burned cars, blasted walls, rubble, wire, and improvised cover.
- `concealment_foliage_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for bush clusters, tree-line breaks, and soft cover edges.
- `road_edge_tiles_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for horizontal and vertical supply route borders.
- `trench_edge_tiles_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for readable trench borders and damaged trench variants.
- `crater_field_tiles_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for shell-pocked open ground.
- `industrial_ruins_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for broken sheds, walls, hard cover, and debris.
- `seasonal_foliage_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for dry, pale, and weathered tree-line dressing.
- `artillery_aftermath_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for scorch, blast scars, churned dirt, and splintered ground.
- `rural_field_tiles_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for crops, dirt lanes, trampled grass, and shallow shell marks.
- `hedgerow_foliage_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for soft cover and field boundaries.
- `weather_overlay_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px transparent sheet for rain streaks, haze, dust, and low-visibility puffs.
- `wet_mud_variants_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px sheet for wet mud, puddles, ruts, and saturated terrain.
- `orchard_foliage_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px sheet for orchard and spaced tree-line foliage.
- Both sheets are loaded by `RaidScene` and placed as static battlefield dressing around camps and the contested middle.
- `woodland_terrain_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px terrain sheet for grass, moss, dirt, mud, leaf litter, log ground, stones, reeds, and tracks.
- `woodland_foliage_32_sheet.png` is an 8 by 4, 32 px transparent foliage sheet for grass tufts, bushes, stumps, and rocks.
- `woodland_trees_96_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 96 px transparent tree sheet with leafy and bare trees.
- `woodland_logs_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px transparent sheet for logs and deadfall.
- `woodland_treeline_128x96_sheet.png` is a 4-frame transparent sheet for map-edge tree lines and soft cover.
- `woodland_deadfall_cover_128x96.png`, `woodland_dense_bushline_128.png`, and `woodland_bare_tree_96.png` are larger standalone woodland cover props.
- `woodland_preview_sheet.png` is a non-runtime visual index for quick review.
- `tree_line_64_sheet.png`, `battlefield_scars_64_sheet.png`, and `village_war_props_64_sheet.png` are indexed shared environment sheets for foliage blockers, terrain scars, and ruined village props.
