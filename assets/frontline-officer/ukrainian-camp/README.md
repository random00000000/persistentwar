# Frontline Officer Ukrainian-Side Camp Art

Top-down fictional player-side camp art for the first-town war slice.

These assets use green field materials with blue/yellow identification panels for fast in-game readability. They do not use real unit marks, official insignia, slogans, or flags.

## Runtime Notes

- `ukrainian_command_core_160x144.png`, `ukrainian_barracks_tent_112x96.png`, `ukrainian_supply_dump_128x112.png`, and `ukrainian_logistics_truck_144x96.png` are loaded by `RaidScene` for camp-a.
- The art is transparent PNG intended for Phaser sprites.
- Camp overlays in `RaidScene` still provide live control/command readout rings over these sprites.
- `ukrainian_camp_modules_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 4, 64 px runtime camp module sheet for generated camp layouts.
- `ukrainian_camp_damage_64_sheet.png` is a 4 by 2, 64 px damaged-state sheet for camp attrition and destroyed-cover reads.
- Officer-war support sheets: `ukrainian_perimeter_defenses_64_sheet.png`, `ukrainian_fieldwork_build_stages_64_sheet.png`, and `ukrainian_logistics_markers_32_sheet.png`.
- Camp interior and supply sheets: `ukrainian_camp_interiors_64_sheet.png`, `ukrainian_supply_stacks_64_sheet.png`, and `ukrainian_signal_panels_32_sheet.png`.
- Heavy and service sheets: `ukrainian_heavy_emplacements_64_sheet.png`, `ukrainian_camp_services_64_sheet.png`, and `ukrainian_ground_markers_32_sheet.png`.
- State and recovery sheets: `ukrainian_abandoned_camp_64_sheet.png` and `ukrainian_recovery_medical_64_sheet.png`.
- Camouflage and night sheets: `ukrainian_camo_command_64_sheet.png` and `ukrainian_night_watch_64_sheet.png`.
